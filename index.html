🍃 Connect to MongoDB Atlas (Cloud)

Your backend is currently live and saving tasks! To connect to your permanent MongoDB Atlas cloud cluster on Vercel:

  1. Open your project in Vercel Dashboard.
  2. Go to Settings > Environment Variables.
  3. Add Variable:
    Key: MONGODB_URI
    Value: mongodb+srv://<user>:<password>@cluster0.xxx.mongodb.net/taskmanager?retryWrites=true&w=majority
  4. Redeploy or promote latest deployment!
